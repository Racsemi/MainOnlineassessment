# PRIVACY.md

## Data Privacy & Retention Policy

RACSEMI Assess collects personally identifiable information (PII) including Candidate Names and Email Addresses.

### Principles
- **Data Minimization**: Only essential PII is collected.
- **Anonymization**: PII is omitted from logging and telemetry.
- **Retention**: Candidate assessment data and PII are retained only as long as necessary for recruitment decisions, after which they should be purged via scheduled maintenance tasks.

### Candidate Telemetry
Candidate browser telemetry (Window blurs, clipboard events) is strictly tied to assessment integrity and does not capture sensitive system information beyond the assessment scope.
